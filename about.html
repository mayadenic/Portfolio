About Me

My name is Maya Denic. I am a current fourth year student at Illinois State University and graduated from Prospect High School. I am a game design major with two minors in music and film studies and am starting my accelerated masters program for game design. I started to do graphic arts my senior year of high school but I had an interest in it since my freshman year. Due to my busy schedule with being in the Prospect Marching Knights, concert band, and symphonic band, I never had the time to fit graphic arts into my schedule. Thankfully, that passion was still in me and I continue to make great works of art. I mostly do Adobe Photoshop work but sometimes I also do vinyl/printing designs on Adobe Illustrator or using my Cricut machine. I do drawings on Procreate and photography when I have the chance. I am currently a media coordinator for Amplify (a club for BRMM members), a media member for the Big Red Marching Machine, a media assistant for Vocal Movement (ISU's only show choirs), and a multimedia member for Redbird Gaming. I hope to design more in the future. Please take a look at all of my artwork so far on the "Gallery" page!
